Introduction

Education, Career, Interests

Welcome to my personal website!

I am a Florida Tech alumnus in Applied Mathematics, with an Applied Area of Computer Science! I am also an experienced Math Tutor. I am hoping to go to Vet School and become a Veterinarian, however, as I feel that helping animals is more of my calling.

Let me start from the beginning:

I was Homeschooled, and I started at Indian River State College (IRSC) at 14 years old. I had a perfect GPA most of my time there, and I started tutoring Calculus (among other maths) there when I was 17. I worked in the Math Lab primarily, but also in classrooms and the Academic Support Center (ASC). I became the president of Delta Mu Epsilon (Math Club). Upon graduating high school, I received my AA in Mathematics from IRSC, with a 3.97 GPA! Because of these things (and my SAT superscore of 1500/1600), I was placed on the 2019 All-Florida Academic Team (which is the top 0.02% of students enrolled in 2 year colleges in Florida).

I was very blessed to have received merit-based, full-ride offers (100% of tuition paid) from multiple universities, including the Florida Institute of Technology, which I decided to attend. During my time there, I was involved in FITSEC (Cybersecurity Club), CPFIT (Competitive Programming Club), and lightly involved in AMS (American Mathematical Society). In December 2023, I received my BS in Applied Mathematics (Applied Area: Computer Science) from FIT, with a 3.68 GPA!

The Summer before I graduated, I was chosen for a competitive internship at none other than Royal Caribbean Group! The internship's focus was using Data Analysis on Cybersecurity trends within the organization. I gained valuable hands-on experience in Power BI, Excel, and Python.

Since completing my BS at FIT, I have returned to be a Math Tutor again at IRSC! I love IRSC so much, I came back to work here a 2nd time, haha! I absolutely love immersing myself in an environment of learning and growth.

I have been doing some reflecting, and ultimately I have decided that my calling is to help animals live a happy and healthy life. I am currently back at IRSC, with a GPA of 3.72 so far, and I am applying to Vet Schools. In the mean time, I am volunteering at H.A.L.O. No-Kill Rescue Shelter, where I bathe dogs, walk dogs, photograph dogs, and socialize dogs. I am also hoping to get an entry-level role that involves working with animals, so that I can be on my way to becoming a Vet. I am very passionate about animal well-being and animal rights, and I hope to specialize in aquatic animals!

Outside of work, I volunteer in a (human) Emergency Department, and I also enjoy spending time with others, doing my hobbies, self-studying, and overall trying to live my best life! I always have a lot going on with my aquatic animals - it's like a fish hospital in my room sometimes, except I'm the only "nurse" so it's constantly short-staffed... going for the realistic look I guess! But, trying to keep my head above water. It fills me with hope when I see new "eggnant" mama shrimp, new fish eggs in the tank, sometimes new baby snails - even just a shrimp molting successfully is a great sign!

And of course, my dogs are extremely clingy, snoutsy, constantly wanting attention and walks - I love saying "yes" to those precious faces! It's so wonderful to come home and feel like a celebrity hounded by the pup-erazzi. Throughout the day, they'll even follow me room-to-room, to be up-close and snuggly! They know who loves them (hint: it's me!). I am also extremely lucky to have a best friend so close to me, who I've been friends with since before 2010! She is the kindest person I know and she is always my inspiration of what a "good" person looks like. Finally, before bed, I track my habits of the day in my spreadsheet. I like having an accountability buddy to help analyze what went wrong during the day and how to fix it. I always want to be improving and being the best version of myself!
